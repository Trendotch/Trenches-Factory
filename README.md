# Tren.ch
# How it works?

To start using **tren.ch**, simply visit the official website and register with your X account to claim your trench profile. There is only one official trench domain: [tren.ch](https://tren.ch)

### What makes tren.ch different?

**Users cannot deploy or trade pre-bond without being a registered user, and only one wallet is allowed per account. Essentially, your X account = your tren.ch wallet.**

### The rules of tren.ch

To ensure the long-term health of the ecosystem, tren.ch has certain unique rules for trading/creating enforced from day one:

- The maximum supply any user can hold of any token pre-bond is **2.5%**, regardless of whether it was bought or received. This guarantees that no one user, even the dev, controls supply.
- Users can only deploy one coin every **60 seconds**, ensuring that developers can’t launch PVP clones alongside their tokens.
- Coins take **60 seconds** to Migrate.
- **No public trading in Block 0** (the first block when creating a coin). This means deployers can no longer bundle tokens without the risk of being front-run by ordinary traders.
- Traders also have more say using tren.ch with a dedicated [Governance](https://docs.tren.ch/governance) wesystem built in to let users vote with their tokens.

To learn more about how to make the most of tren.ch, refer to the rest of the documentation. Good luck trading and creating with [tren.ch](https://tren.ch)!
